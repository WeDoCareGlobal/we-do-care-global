## Evidence required before production claims

- Versioned release, commit SHA, and deployment provenance.
- Reproducible integration tests and CI links.
- Versioned benchmarks, data sources, and methodology.
- Security review scope, access control, and audit trail verification.
- Uptime, incident response, and service ownership evidence.
