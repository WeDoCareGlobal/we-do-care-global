Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Emir Perla · We Do Care Global · Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly when you submit an inquiry via the contact form, including your name, company, business email, and project context. We do not collect credentials, API tokens, or confidential data.

2. How we use your data

Your contact details are used solely to respond to your inquiry. Enquiries are transmitted via FormSubmit, a third-party service hosted in the European Union. Your data is not used for marketing, profiling, or any secondary purpose.

3. Analytics

Each page automatically sends an anonymous page-view event to an analytics endpoint (wdcg-analytics.emirperla96.workers.dev/pv). The payload contains only:

  • The request path (page being viewed)
  • The HTTP referrer (the page that linked to this page)

No IP address, user agent, cookie identifiers, or personal data are transmitted. The endpoint does not set cookies.

4. Cookies and third-party services

This website does not use cookies. We do not serve third-party scripts other than the anonymous analytics endpoint described above. Forms are handled by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold. Contact us at emir@wedocare-global.com and we will respond within 72 hours.

6. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The last-updated date above indicates the current version.
